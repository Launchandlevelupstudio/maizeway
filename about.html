How I work.

The MaizeWay Method, as personal practice.

  • 01

    Discover

    Objectives, constraints, stakeholders, what's already been tried.

    You get: Engagement brief and success criteria

    Week 1

  • 02

    Diagnose

    Where the work is actually breaking down — process, ownership, sequence, or capacity.

    You get: Findings report with prioritized root causes

    Weeks 1–2

  • 03

    Architect

    Design the operating model that fits your constraints.

    You get: Operating model, RACI, sequenced plan, governance and reporting design

    Weeks 2–4

  • 04

    Execute

    Lead the work through delivery; adjust as reality intervenes.

    You get: Weekly reporting, a managed risk register, decisions driven to close

    Weeks 4–12

  • 05

    Hand Off

    Transfer the structure to your team.

    You get: SOPs, templates, documented governance, a transition session

    Final 2 weeks

    • Most engagements move through all five stages in 30 to 90 days. The scope of an engagement is the initiative, not the calendar.
